# KFE — Field-Level Contract Boundary — Pass 10

**Status:** WORKING DESIGN — NOT FROZEN

## Purpose

Define the common field-level contract principles for authoritative KFE records before physical schema implementation.

## Scope

Work, Performance and Admin only. No Timeline. OCR permanently excluded.

## Identity

Every authoritative record has a stable, globally unique identity generated by KFE rather than by a database provider. IDs must remain stable across backup/restore, device transfer and future synchronization.

## Lifecycle metadata

Authoritative records carry the lifecycle metadata needed to reconstruct history, including creation/update information and explicit lifecycle state where the domain requires it. Deletion/correction semantics are domain-owned and must preserve historical integrity.

## Monetary values

Money is represented in paise at the authoritative persistence boundary. UI formatting may convert paise to display currency, but display values are never the financial authority.

## Time

Authoritative operational timestamps are stored in an unambiguous machine-readable representation with sufficient precision for ordering and reconstruction. Local display timezone/format is presentation concern.

## Quantities and measurements

Distance, odometer and fuel quantities use explicit units. A record must never depend on an implicit UI unit. Derived quantities remain reconstructable from authoritative inputs.

## Relationships

References between authoritative records use stable IDs. A child record must not duplicate the parent's business authority. Referential rules are enforced at the domain/application/persistence boundaries appropriate to the relationship.

## Work records

Work contracts must be able to represent:
- work/session identity and lifecycle
- business/personal scope
- breaks
- trip/ride facts
- ride timing, distance, fare, operator/status and cancellation where applicable
- pickup/drop information where captured
- odometer facts
- optional source evidence reference without making evidence authoritative

## Vehicle and driver

Vehicle and driver records own identity and lifecycle/configuration facts. Historical references must remain interpretable when configuration changes over time.

## Financial/operational records

Fuel, maintenance, loan and revenue records retain their authoritative inputs and relevant effective dates. Calculated outputs must identify their calculation context/version where required for historical reconstruction.

## Configuration

Configuration that affects historical business interpretation must be effective-dated. Current configuration must not silently rewrite historical meaning.

## Derived data

Performance metrics, dashboards, summaries, notifications and other read models are derived representations. They may carry timestamps/snapshots for display or audit where explicitly required, but do not become alternate business authority.

## Sync/backup compatibility

Stable identity, lifecycle metadata and authoritative timestamps must be sufficient to support future backup/restore and synchronization without changing business identity.

## Explicit non-contract authorities

Database-generated IDs, UI state, form fields before validation, provider SDK objects, cloud records, sync envelopes, notification payloads, cached data, screenshots and read models are not authoritative business contracts.

## Open field-level questions

1. Exact field names/types for each record.
2. Required vs optional status for each field.
3. Exact lifecycle states and transition rules.
4. Exact relationship cardinalities.
5. Exact calculation-version metadata requirements.
6. Exact source-evidence retention policy.

These must be resolved before physical schema implementation is considered ready.
